This site doesn't serve the download. Once you're in the beta, we email you a private link to the Mac app, the Google Play test link for your phone, and your beta token.

Version
2.0.1
Mac
Apple silicon only (M1 or later), macOS 11.0 (Big Sur) or later. Intel Macs aren't supported.
Android
Android 10 or later, from Google Play's testing programme (invited Google accounts only). There is no APK download.

Ask for a place

  1. Check your Mac. The beta runs only on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later): Apple menu › About This Mac shows “Chip”. If it shows “Processor”, it's an Intel Mac and the beta won't run on it. You also need an Android phone on Android 10 or later.
  2. Email us. The button opens your email app with a message to chetanpujari.sitit@gmail.com that asks for four things: your name, your Mac's chip, your phone model, and the Google account email you use on that phone. We add that Google account to Sidekick's Google Play test list, because only accounts on that list can install the Android app.
  3. Wait for our reply. We read each request ourselves; nothing is automatic. If there's a place, we email you a private link to the Mac app, the Google Play test link for your phone, and your beta token.
Email us to request access

If the button doesn't open an email app, write to chetanpujari.sitit@gmail.com with the subject “Sidekick beta: request access” and:

  • Your name
  • Your Mac's chip (Apple menu > About This Mac > Chip; the beta runs on Apple silicon only, M1 or later)
  • Your Android phone model (Android 10 or later)
  • The Google account email you use on that phone (we add it to the Google Play test list)

By sending the email you agree to us using those details to run the beta, as described in Privacy. This site doesn't store anything: your email goes from your email app to our inbox. Don't send passwords or your beta token.
