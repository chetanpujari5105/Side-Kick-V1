Getting started

  1. Download Sidekick for your Mac with the button above.Sidekick for your Mac from the private link the person who invited you sent.
  2. Open it. Open the .dmg, drag Sidekick into Applications, then open Sidekick from Applications. macOS says it could not verify the app: choose Done. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Sidekick and choose Open Anyway, enter your Mac password, then choose Open Anyway again in the dialog that follows. You do this once, and you never need to turn off Gatekeeper.
  3. Allow the keychain once. Sidekick shows a short note first: choose Continue. macOS may then ask for your Mac password for “Sidekick Safe Storage”. Type it and choose Always Allow. After you install a newer test build, macOS asks once more.
  4. Enter your beta token. Copy it from our email “Your Sidekick beta token”Copy the token you were sent, then press Paste token. It's a long piece of text that starts with SIDEKICK-TRIAL-1, and it's not the checksum above. If you copy the whole email, Sidekick finds the token inside it. The token is checked on your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere.
  5. Allow your local network. When macOS asks whether Sidekick may find devices on your local network, choose Allow. Sidekick then shows a code to scan.
  6. Pair your phone. On the phone, open Sidekick and tap Get started, then on Connect your Mac tap Scan the code and point it at the code on your Mac.
